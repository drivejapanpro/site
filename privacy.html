Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect or store personal information such as your name, email address, or contact details. We also do not store your learning progress, test results, or performance data on our servers. However, certain non-personal data may be automatically collected by third-party services integrated into the app.

2. How the App Functions

All practice tests and learning features operate within the app. Your activity and progress are not stored or tracked by us. Any temporary data used during app operation is not retained by our systems.

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services such as Google Analytics and Google AdMob. These services may automatically collect certain information, including device information, usage data, and advertising identifiers, to help us understand app performance and provide advertisements. This data is handled according to their respective privacy policies.

4. How Third-Party Data Is Used

Google Analytics helps us analyze app usage and improve functionality. Google AdMob is used to display advertisements, which may be personalized or non-personalized based on your device settings. We do not access or control the data collected by these services.

5. Data Storage and Security

We do not store user data on our own servers. Data collected by third-party services is managed and secured by those providers. While we choose reputable partners, we cannot guarantee absolute security of data handled by third parties.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or share any data collected by us. Any data collected by third-party services is processed and used in accordance with their own policies.

7. Your Choices

You can control certain data collection through your device settings, such as limiting ad personalization or resetting your advertising identifier. You may also opt out of certain analytics tracking where supported by your device or platform.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the app. Continued use of the app after updates indicates your acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Disclaimer

This app is intended for educational and practice purposes only. We do not guarantee that use of this app will result in passing any official driving test. Users should refer to official sources for accurate and up-to-date driving regulations and requirements.
